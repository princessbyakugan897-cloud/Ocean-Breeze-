MOUNTAIN ESCAPE

Mt. Pulag — Benguet/Ifugao/Nueva Vizcaya Philippines

Mount Pulag is famous for its breathtaking "sea of clouds" and majestic sunrise, making it one of the most popular and rewarding hiking destinations in the Philippines.

NATURE

Mount Mayon — Albay, Bicol Region, Philippines

Mount Mayon is famous all over the world for its nearly perfect symmetrical cone shape — that’s why it’s called the most beautiful volcano on Earth!

UNDERGROUND WONDER

Puerto Princesa — Palawan, Philippines

Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature! This incredible underground river winds through dark caves filled with amazing rock formations before flowing out into the sea.
